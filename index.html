SATELLITE AI/ML · PROJECT WORKFLOW

From cloud pattern to cyclone outlook

The connected dashboard provides weather, river, marine and historical-track context. Satellite imagery and a trained cyclone model are the next implementation stage.

MODEL STATUS · DESIGN STAGE
01
OBSERVE

Satellite image sequence

Use INSAT-3D/3DR infrared and water-vapour channels to follow cloud-top temperature and storm structure over time.

IMD satellite products ↗
02
IDENTIFY

Find the storm centre

A vision model can locate an organized rotating cloud system and estimate its centre with a confidence score.

Planned: image detection and centre localization
03
CLASSIFY

Describe its pattern

Candidate labels: organizing, symmetric or eye-like, sheared or asymmetric, and weakening. These need expert-reviewed training labels.

Pattern tags are not official storm categories
04
PREDICT

Estimate the next change

A time-series model could combine image sequences, storm history and environmental data to estimate track and intensity trend with uncertainty.

Planned: 6, 12 and 24-hour guidance
HISTORICAL SATELLITE TRAINING DATA

NOAA HURSAT-B1 offers storm-centred infrared imagery at 3-hourly, about 8 km resolution through 2015. It is a historical training source, not a live feed.
