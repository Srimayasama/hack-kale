FOUR-STEP TRACK OUTLOOK

Where could this system go?

Four forecast steps show the ensemble centre, modelled wind band, central pressure, and Odisha coastal reference points close to the projected track.

Track proximity is a screening view: a listed location is within 150 km of at least one ensemble track at that lead. It is not an affected-area boundary, landfall forecast, or probability. Wind bands are indicative comparisons against IMD thresholds using ECMWF model maximum 10 m wind, not official IMD cyclone classifications. A model system ID is not a cyclone name, and the same ID is not guaranteed to be the same physical storm across ensemble members. Follow IMD and OSDMA warnings.

ATMOSSCALE AI/ML · TRAINED CYCLONE MODEL

Historical cyclone data in. Four-stage forecast out.

AtmosScale now trains its own Bay of Bengal model from historical cyclone tracks. It forecasts centre movement, wind and pressure, estimates a 34-kt wind radius when data allow, then compares that footprint with Odisha coastal reference points.

TRACK MODEL · TRAINED
01
TRAIN

Learn from past storms

Use NOAA IBTrACS Bay of Bengal tracks from 1980–2024. Each training example contains past centre positions, wind and pressure.

Later cyclone seasons are kept out for a separate holdout score
02
PREDICT

Follow its future path

Extra Trees regression predicts centre movement at four lead times. The live AIFS track supplies the first 24 hours of motion context.

Live view: AtmosScale prediction is drawn beside AIFS guidance
03
ESTIMATE

Wind and pressure

Separate regressors estimate future sustained wind and central pressure. The wind-based storm band is derived from the wind estimate.

Historical wind sources can use different averaging periods
04
MAP SIZE

Show a wind-radius estimate

Where enough best-track labels exist, the model estimates the radius of 34-kt winds and draws a circle around the forecast centre.

Reference locations are screening points, not confirmed impact zones
MODEL TRAINING SOURCE
